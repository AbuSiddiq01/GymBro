Privacy Policy

GymBro · Last updated: October 2026

This policy explains what information GymBro collects, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

If you sign in with Google, we receive only your name and email address from your Google account. We never see your Google password, and we do not access Google Drive, Gmail, Calendar or any other Google service.

If you sign up with a phone number, we collect your name, phone number and the password you choose.

While you use the app, we also store the fitness data you enter, such as workouts, attendance, profile photo and bio.

How we use it

  • To create your account and sign you in.
  • To save and show your workouts, progress and profile.
  • To contact you about your account if needed.

We do not sell your information or use it for advertising.

Where it is stored

Your data is stored with our database provider, Supabase. Sign-in with Google is handled through Google and Supabase.

Sharing

We do not share your personal information with third parties, except with the service providers that run the app (such as Supabase) or when the law requires it. Other members can see the profile details you choose to make public in the app.

Deleting your data

You can ask us to delete your account and all related data at any time by emailing us at the address below. We will remove it within a reasonable time.

Contact

Changes
